Scientific name, synonym and stolons: follow the references

The name accepted by Kew POWO

Kew Plants of the World Online: Digitaria eriantha lists Digitaria eriantha Steud. as an accepted name. Its Digitaria decumbens entry treats Digitaria decumbens Stent as a synonym of Digitaria eriantha.

Stoloniferous growth

Feedipedia: Pangola grass and Bruce G. Cook, Pangola grass fact sheet, Nov 2007 describe stolon-bearing forms in which stolons may extend along or near the ground and may root at the nodes. This reports the qualified meaning of the cited sources; it does not guarantee rooting, establishment or yield in any field.
