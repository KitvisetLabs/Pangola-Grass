How do Pangola product types differ?

กิจวิเศษแพงโกล่า / Kitviset Pangola lists fresh Pangola grass, baled Pangola hay and two types of planting material: stem cuttings and uprooted clumps with roots. Listing these categories does not confirm stock or a collection date.

Identify what you need before asking

Fresh grass and hay are different product forms. For planting material, specify either stems cut into sections or clumps/plants lifted with roots for transplanting. Do not group these names together in a way that suggests they are the same material.

What to tell the seller

State the product form, quantity needed and date you want to ask about. Before deciding, have the seller confirm the selling unit, details for that particular order, price and collection point. This page is not a quotation and does not state transport charges.
