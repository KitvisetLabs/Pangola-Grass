Quick questions before contacting กิจวิเศษแพงโกล่า

What are the two planting materials?

Stem cuttings are stems cut into sections. Rooted clumps are clumps or plants lifted with roots for transplanting. Do not use these terms interchangeably or call either material seeds.

Does Grade A mean certification?

In the seller's baled hay information, this is the seller's own term, not external standards certification or an analysis result.

Does a product description mean it is available?

No. Ask directly about your intended order, including price, selling unit and collection arrangements. Old photographs or messages do not confirm current stock.

Are the shop and field in the same place?

No. The shop is at Kitviset Rice Mill in Maha Sarakham. The field pin shows Ban Du, Na Pho, Buriram. Call 093-069-8712 to confirm the point and time before travelling.

Are the page and group the same?

Use the business's Facebook page for inquiries with the business. The linked group is for discussion/trading among members; it is not stated to belong to or be managed by the shop.

Is this animal-feeding advice?

No. This page does not give feed formulations, feeding quantities or nutritional analysis results. Animal names or search terms are not evidence that a product is suitable.
