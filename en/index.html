What to ask when hay offer details conflict

If prices, selling units or weights differ between messages about a hay offer, do not simply select one figure. Ask the seller to reconfirm the offer for the purchase you intend to make. This article is a checklist, not a quotation.

1. Confirm the product

Ask which form of baled Pangola hay the offer covers and which batch or order it refers to. Grade A is the seller's term, not evidence of external certification.

2. Confirm the unit and weighing method

Ask whether pricing is per bale or by weight. If a weight is provided, clarify whether it is estimated or measured and whether it applies to the product under discussion. Do not turn an old price into a current price.

3. Request separate cost details

Have the seller confirm the product price, total and other agreed expenses. If transport is needed, ask about feasibility and conditions for this particular order. Old messages alone do not confirm a service or transport rate.

4. Confirm collection before travelling

Call 093-069-8712 to confirm the collection point and time. The shop and field are separate locations. Neither a map pin nor this article confirms that you can collect goods without an appointment.
