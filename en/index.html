Planting methods described by the owner

Cut stems: scatter the cut stems onto muddy ground, then have people tread on them so that some parts are submerged in the mud and others remain exposed.

Whole grass clumps lifted with roots intact for transplanting: transplant them in a way similar to planting rice seedlings.

These are the owner's descriptions only, not agronomic advice or a guarantee of results.

When hay-sale information conflicts, what should you ask to correct before agreeing?

If the price, sales unit, or weight in a sales message for compressed dried pangola grass does not match, ask the seller for a corrected summary of the items before confirming the purchase. Do not change the price unit yourself or assume that an omitted field means there is no charge. Clearly separate what is confirmed, what is an estimate, and what is still awaiting an answer.

For people collecting information for a farm’s purchase approver, this article is for checking received messages or documents. It is not a quotation form approved by the shop. If you find conflicting information, try the following questions.

The price unit in the document and the chat message does not match. Which one should be used?

Do not choose one yourself. Ask: “What unit is the price for the item we are agreeing to calculated by, and which version of the message should be used for confirmation?” Ask the seller to provide the product name, quantity, sales unit, and price in full for the same item.

The price unit previously provided by this seller for compressed dried hay was per bale. Therefore, do not relabel that figure as a price per kilogram. If the document you received uses a different unit, ask for fresh confirmation. Do not use this article as a substitute for the agreement for the purchase round being considered.

If the weight is only an estimate but has been entered as the actual weight, how should it be corrected?

Separate an “estimated weight” field from a “weight weighed for the lot to be received” field, and ask whether weighing data for that lot is already available. If it is not, keep the status as “awaiting confirmation of weighed weight” rather than entering an estimate as the actual weight.

The per-bale weight previously stated by the seller was an estimate, not a confirmed weighing result for every bale. Therefore, do not use that old figure as evidence of the weight of a new lot, and do not divide by it to create your own selling price per kilogram.

The document says Grade A, but the quality-evidence field is blank. How should this be recorded?

For compressed dried pangola grass from กิจวิเศษแพงโกล่า (Kitviset Pangola), Grade A is the seller’s term. It is not an external standard or a laboratory analysis result. Record it as “Grade A according to the seller’s term,” without changing it to “certified.”

If the purchase approver needs additional quality information, ask what information about the lot to be purchased is available for consideration. If it has not yet been received, state “additional information not yet received.” Do not fill in test results or product characteristics on the seller’s behalf.

The shipping-cost field is blank. Can it be treated as delivered, with shipping included?

Do not yet assume that delivery is included. The price previously provided by the seller did not include shipping. If delivery is wanted, first ask whether delivery to the required destination is available for this round. Then ask the seller to separate the product price, shipping cost, and seller-confirmed total. If it has not yet been agreed, mark it as “pending confirmation” instead of entering zero.

If you choose self-pickup, confirm the location and time by phone before setting out. Do not treat the shop pin or farm pin as confirmation of a pickup appointment.

The information has been corrected several times. What should be sent to the purchase approver?

Ask the seller to send the summary that will be used for the agreement once more, with the date and the items that were corrected. Then compile the information from that version. Do not combine a price from an old message with the quantity or conditions from a new message.

You can use this short message:

“Please summarize the items to be used for confirmation this time, stating the product name, quantity, sales unit, price, whether the weight is an estimate or has been weighed, and the pickup or delivery conditions. If there is a shipping cost, please show it separately, and please confirm which parts of the previous message are no longer applicable. Please also identify anything that has not yet been confirmed.”

Before forwarding it to the purchase approver, separate the headings Confirmed / Estimated / Awaiting an answer. If there are still conflicts, ask for clarification before confirming the purchase. Do not summarize on the seller’s behalf.

The product information above refers to what the seller previously stated. This article is not a quotation and does not confirm the current price, weight, stock, or delivery service. Confirm the details for the round being agreed directly with the seller.
