Clumps lifted with roots for transplanting

Rooted planting material in the information from กิจวิเศษแพงโกล่า / Kitviset Pangola means clumps or plants lifted with roots for transplanting. It should not be understood as isolated root fragments or cut stem sections, and should not be called seeds.

Before making an inquiry

Specify material lifted with roots. Ask the seller to confirm the characteristics of the material under discussion, selling unit and details for that order. If you want a quantity estimate, explain your site's area and conditions. Do not replace site-specific advice with old estimates.

The owner's explanation

The owner describes transplanting by inserting the plants into the ground, in a manner similar to transplanting rice. This reports the owner's explanation; it does not guarantee establishment, survival rate or yield. See the separate planting-methods article for details.

Example message: “I am interested in Pangola clumps lifted with roots. Please explain the material, selling unit and details for my intended order. I will also need to confirm a collection appointment before travelling.”
