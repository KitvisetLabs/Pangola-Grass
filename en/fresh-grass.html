Fresh Pangola grass: clarify details before agreeing

Fresh Pangola grass is one of the product categories described by กิจวิเศษแพงโกล่า / Kitviset Pangola. This page helps you prepare questions; it is not an announcement that grass is ready for collection or delivery.

Checklist before contacting the seller

Specify fresh grass, not hay or planting material. State the quantity and date you are interested in, then ask which details can be agreed for that occasion. Ask the seller to clarify the selling unit and weighing method instead of guessing from photographs or old messages.

Before arranging collection

Confirm the price and order total separately from other expenses. If transport is discussed, ask about the conditions directly. This page does not confirm any carrier, delivery area or transport charge.

Cattle or buffalo keepers asking about fresh grass can use the same checklist. However, this page does not prescribe a ration, feeding quantity, nutritional value or suitability for an individual animal.

Example message: “I am interested in fresh Pangola grass. Please clarify the details for my desired date, the selling unit, weighing method and collection point that I need to confirm by phone.”
