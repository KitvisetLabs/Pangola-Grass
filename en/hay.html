Baled Pangola hay: understand the term Grade A

The listed product category is baled Pangola hay. กิจวิเศษแพงโกล่า / Kitviset Pangola uses “Grade A” as the seller's own term, not as external standards certification or a laboratory analysis result.

A label does not replace evidence for each batch

If you have specific purchasing requirements, ask what information can be checked for that batch. Do not infer weight, nutritional value, suitability for animals or availability from the term Grade A or a photograph of a bale.

Ask about selling units before comparing offers

Ask the seller whether the offer is priced per bale or by weight, whether any stated weight is estimated or measured, and which expenses are included. If two messages conflict, request clear updated details rather than calculating comparable unit prices from old figures.

When asking, specify baled Pangola hay; do not replace the product name with “baled straw.” If you are unsure what is being offered, ask the seller to confirm the product name directly.
