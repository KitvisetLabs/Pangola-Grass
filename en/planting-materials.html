Name the two Pangola planting materials clearly

กิจวิเศษแพงโกล่า / Kitviset Pangola distinguishes two forms of planting material: stems cut into sections, and clumps/plants lifted with roots for transplanting. These terms should not be used interchangeably.

Stem cuttings

These are sections cut from the stem. When asking, specify “stem cuttings”; do not use the word seeds to refer to this material.

Uprooted clumps with roots

These are clumps or plants lifted with their roots for transplanting. This is not another name for stem cuttings, and seeds is not the correct term for this material either.

Before selecting a material

Tell the seller which type you want and explain your site's conditions. If the terms are still unclear, ask the seller to explain the material being offered again. This page does not specify application rates per area, guarantee establishment or confirm that planting material is available.
