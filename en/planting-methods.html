Owner-described planting methods, separated by material

This text reports the method explained by the owner of กิจวิเศษแพงโกล่า / Kitviset Pangola. It is not a certified agricultural manual and does not guarantee planting results. First confirm whether you are using stem cuttings or clumps lifted with roots.

Stem cuttings: two steps in the owner's explanation

  1. Scatter the stem cuttings onto mud.
  2. Have people step on them so that some parts are submerged and some remain exposed.

“Some parts submerged, some exposed” does not specify a fixed depth. This page does not add unverified planting depths, spacing or quantities per area.

Clumps lifted with roots

The owner describes transplanting by inserting the plants into the ground, similarly to transplanting rice. This explanation concerns material lifted with roots; it is not a substitute for the explanation about stem cuttings.
