What are Pangola stem cuttings?

In the information provided by กิจวิเศษแพงโกล่า / Kitviset Pangola, stem cuttings are stems cut into sections for planting. They are not seeds and are not clumps lifted with roots.

Questions to establish a shared understanding

Start by specifying stem cuttings. Ask about the material the seller means, the selling unit and details that can be confirmed for that order. If discussing quantity in relation to land area, first provide information about your site. Do not treat old estimates as a standard rate.

Separate material selection from the planting method

The planting-methods page describes the owner's explanation of using stem cuttings on mud. It reports the owner's method, rather than advice guaranteed to work on every site or a promise of results.

Example message: “I am interested in Pangola stem cuttings, not uprooted clumps with roots. Please explain the material, selling unit and the information I should prepare about my planting site.”
